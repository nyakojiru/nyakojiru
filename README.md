### Principles

- **Privacy-first** — local-first, zero telemetry. Nothing phones home unless you opt in.
- **Cypherpunk** — secrets stay encrypted on your machine; sync is self-hosted or end-to-end encrypted, never someone else's cleartext.
- **Open source** — permissive licenses (MIT and friends) are welcome; no copyleft required.
- **KISS** — keep it simple, stupid: one simple way to do each thing.

In practice: my tools never impersonate official clients or read other apps' data (browser cookies included), and anything unofficial is documented as unofficial.
